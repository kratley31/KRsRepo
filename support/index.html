How can we help?

Report a problem or suggest a feature. Your message opens in your email app, addressed to info@sottoapps.com — just hit send.

Please enter your email so we can reply to you.
For bugs, include your device and iOS version if you can. Please add a few details before sending.

Prefer to write it yourself? Email info@sottoapps.com directly.

Your email app should have opened with your message ready. If it didn’t, email info@sottoapps.com directly — nothing is sent until you press send.

We only use your email to reply to you. See the privacy policy.
