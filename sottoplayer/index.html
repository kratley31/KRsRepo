Most players announce what you’re into. Sotto never says a word.

Private by design

What you play is nobody’s business but yours.

No account to make. No profile to build. Nothing leaves your phone unless you send it there yourself.

  • Hide art everywhereLock screen, Control Center, even AirPlay. Your phone broadcasts only what you allow.
  • Nothing phones homeNo telemetry, no trackers. The only network Sotto touches is one you point it at.
  • On your deviceNo analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs. Files, bookmarks and progress stay local.

Make it yours

Dress the library the way you want it seen.

Swap any cover for something only you understand — a single photo, a rotating pool of images, a live visualizer, or just a well-chosen emoji. Set it per category, or per title.

  • Your own imageOne cover for everything, or a pool that gently rotates.
  • Live visualizerChoose a style and palette; let the sound draw the cover.
  • An icon or emojiDozens to pick from when a face says it better than a cover.

Built for the long listen

Everything a spoken-word listener actually reaches for.

Hours-long books and shows have their own needs. Sotto sweats those details.

Smart resumeRewinds a few seconds on play so you never lose the thread.
ChaptersJump by chapter, with seek scoped to where you are.
Variable speedSeparate defaults for books and music, tuned to taste.
Fading sleep timerEases the volume down instead of cutting off mid-sentence.
Full equalizerPresets plus dedicated bass and vocal boost.
Room reverbOptional audio-environment space for a roomier sound.

Smart bookmarks

Marks that remember the words.

Tap once and Sotto transcribes the last few seconds — entirely on-device — so a bookmark isn’t just a timestamp, it’s the sentence you wanted to keep. Auto-save on pause, and export the lot to a clean PDF.

Your library, your way

Bring your own — and keep it in sync, privately.

Import your own files, subscribe by search or paste any RSS feed, or connect a self-hosted Audiobookshelf server. Optional iCloud sync carries bookmarks and progress across your devices through your account — never ours.

  • Your own filesImport audio straight in — it plays through Sotto’s full pipeline.
  • Podcasts & RSSSearch and subscribe, or add any feed. Fresh episodes refresh in the background.
  • AudiobookshelfConnect your self-hosted server; downloads stay on-device.
  • Private iCloud syncBookmarks and progress follow you — stored in your iCloud, not a server we run.
